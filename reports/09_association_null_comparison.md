# FIREWATCH AI — Stage 10: Candidate association null comparison

**Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Status:** Completed exploratory conditional null analysis; not a validation study.

## Question

How much of the October 2025 MODIS–VIIRS candidate-edge count could be generated by spatial coincidence under each sensor's observed daily location density and observation-time distribution?

## Null design

- Study slice: October 2025 `archive` stream only, 4,727 normalized records across Aqua, Terra, SNPP, N20, and N21.
- For each replicate, within each `satellite × UTC day` group, randomly permute observed `(latitude, longitude)` pairs among the original records. Keep each record's ID, sensor/product, timestamp, stream, and validation state fixed.
- This preserves the daily location multiset and timestamp distribution for every platform while breaking the original within-day coordinate-to-time assignment.
- Count only `MODIS-VIIRS` candidate edges at three exploratory thresholds: 0.5 km/1 h, 1 km/3 h, and 2 km/6 h.
- 29 permutations; NumPy generator seed `20261114`; empirical upper-tail fraction `(1 + number of null counts >= observed)/(29 + 1)`.
- Raw permutation output is `data/processed/null_comparison_oct2025.csv`; reproducible notebook is `notebooks/08_association_null_comparison.ipynb`.

## Results

| Distance / time | Observed edges | Null mean (SD) | Null 2.5–97.5% quantiles | Observed / null mean | Empirical upper-tail fraction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5 km / 1 h | 24 | 22.72 (1.00) | 21.0–24.3 | 1.056 | 0.233 |
| 1 km / 3 h | 332 | 321.86 (2.80) | 317.0–326.6 | 1.031 | 0.033 |
| 2 km / 6 h | 909 | 902.14 (6.73) | 892.0–912.5 | 1.008 | 0.233 |

At 1 km / 3 h, all 29 null draws were below the observed count (null maximum 328), but the contrast is only about 10 edges above a mean near 322. The estimator’s minimum nonzero tail resolution is 1/30. The narrower and broader thresholds did not show the same tail pattern. These data do not justify selecting 1 km / 3 h as an optimal match rule.

## Interpretation and limits

The conditional null reproduces most of the candidate-edge count. This indicates that raw proximity counts are strongly affected by the source-specific daily spatial concentration and sensor activity, and that a naive candidate count overstates how much cross-sensor evidence is attributable to specific coordinate-time pairing.

The null is deliberately narrow. It does not represent scan geometry, pixel footprints, revisit timing, geolocation error, detection probability, fire growth, sensor dependence, or true fire identity. Preserving daily spatial density can retain shared fire-landscape hotspots in the null, so this is a conservative conditional comparison for the coordinate-time pairing question. A small empirical tail fraction is not match precision, probability that a candidate is true, or event-detection performance. It is not corrected for examining three threshold settings.

## Decisions and next validation

- Keep candidate links as an inspectable evidence graph; do not deduplicate or cluster observations based on this result.
- Do not fit an anomaly model or present a harmonized fire-event count.
- Next validation should add independent event/perimeter labels or sensor-overpass/coverage context, then compare pre-registered association rules and null designs over multiple periods.
- Extend null analysis to other seasons/periods only after defining a more complete sensor-observation process; do not extrapolate this October result to all dates.
